# CipherRoll — Help & FAQ (in-app + website)

Plain, honest copy. Each question has a **slug** for the analytics event `help_article_opened`. Items marked **[DEV CONFIRM]** must be checked against the real build before the text ships. Do not use "encrypted", "military-grade" or "nobody can see" until the security review passes. Updated 2 Nov 2026 (v2).

## Getting started
| Slug | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| what-is | What is CipherRoll? | A private vault on your iPhone for photos, videos, files and notes. It unlocks with Face ID, a PIN, a pattern or a password. |
| need-account | Do I need an account? | No. The vault works without any account. An account is only needed for the invite and reward feature. |
| add-items | How do I add things? | Tap **Add**, choose Photos, Videos, Files or Notes, then pick what to import. You can also use Share in other apps (see below). |
| photo-access | Does CipherRoll need access to all my photos? | No. You choose the photos to add, so no full photo access is needed. Access is only asked if you want CipherRoll to offer deleting the originals afterwards. Choose Selected Photos if you prefer. |
| originals | Are my originals deleted when I import? | No. After import we ask if you want to delete the originals from Photos. You decide. Deleted photos stay in "Recently Deleted" in Photos for about 30 days. With iCloud Photos on, they are removed on your other devices too. |
| hide-from-photos | Why do my photos still show in the Photos app? | The originals stay there until you delete them. Use "Review and delete" after importing. |
| vaults | What is a vault? | A separate space with its own lock, for example Personal and Work. Free plan has 1 vault. |

## Locking and unlocking
| Slug | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| lock-types | How can I lock a vault? | PIN (at least 6 digits), pattern, password, custom phrase (8+ characters), or a calculator-style PIN. Face ID can be added as a shortcut. |
| face-id | Is Face ID the only lock? | No. Face ID (or Touch ID on devices that use it) is a shortcut. If it fails, you use your PIN, pattern or password. |
| lock-first | What do I see when I open the app? | The unlock method you chose for that vault, right away. If you use Face ID, it starts at once, and your PIN is always there as a backup. |
| lock-timing | When does the app lock? | Every time you come back to it. In Settings you can choose Immediately, after 1 minute or after 5 minutes. The app switcher preview is hidden. |
| change-lock | Can I change my lock later? | Yes. Open the vault, then Settings, then Lock. |
| calc-pin | What is the calculator-style PIN? | An optional unlock screen that looks like a simple calculator. You type your PIN and press =. It helps when someone glances at your screen. It is a privacy option in a vault app, not a real calculator. Turn it on in Settings, then Lock. |
| break-in | What is the break-in selfie? | An optional setting, off until you turn it on. If someone enters a wrong PIN several times, the front camera takes a photo. The photo stays on this iPhone, in your vault, and is not uploaded. It needs camera permission. |

## Decoy vault (Pro)
| Slug | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| decoy-what | What is a decoy vault? | A second vault with its own PIN. If someone makes you unlock your iPhone, the decoy PIN opens a different vault with different items. |
| decoy-separate | Is the decoy stored separately? | Yes. It has its own PIN, keys and items. Nothing is copied from your real vault. |
| decoy-forgot | What if I forget the decoy PIN? | Its items cannot be recovered. The recovery key does not cover the decoy. Keep only harmless items there. |
| decoy-limits | Will it fool everyone? | It helps in a casual check. It is not a guarantee against a determined person. We say this so you can decide. |

## Forgot your PIN
| Slug | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| recovery-key | What is the recovery key? | A code made on your iPhone when you set up. It opens your vaults if you forget your PIN. We cannot see it or reset your PIN for you. It does not recover deleted items, and it cannot restore files if you lose this iPhone. |
| pin-length | Why do I need at least 6 digits? | Short PINs are easy to guess. You can choose more digits, or a longer passcode with letters, for more strength. |
| lost-key | I lost both my PIN and my recovery key. | We cannot open your vault for you. Your items stay locked. This is why we ask you to test and save the key during setup. |
| key-where | Where should I keep my recovery key? | Somewhere safe that is not Photos: a password manager, a printed copy or Files. |

## Your data and privacy
| Slug | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| where-stored | Where are my items stored? | On this iPhone, inside the app. Vault contents stay on this iPhone. CipherRoll does not upload your vault items. **[DEV CONFIRM storage + protection method]** |
| encryption | How is my vault protected? | **[Write after security review. Until then: "Your vault is protected by your lock and by iOS file protection."]** |
| search | Does search send my data anywhere? | No. Search runs on this iPhone. |
| new-iphone | What happens when I get a new iPhone? | Vault items do not move by themselves. Before switching, use **Export all items**, then import them on the new iPhone. Cloud backup is not available yet. |
| delete-app | What if I delete the app? | Your vault items are deleted with it. Make a backup first (Settings, Export a backup) if you want to keep them. |
| lose-iphone | What if I lose or replace my iPhone? | Your vault stays on this iPhone only. If it is lost or replaced, the files are gone, even if you still have the recovery key. Make a backup first: Settings, Export a backup. |
| ipad | Does CipherRoll work on iPad? | Yes. One app works on iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Each device keeps its own vault. Items do not sync between devices. Use Export a backup and Restore to move items. |
| backup-what | How do I keep a copy of my vault? | Settings, Export a backup. Choose a password, then save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, or share it to your computer. Keep the file and the password in different places. |
| backup-forgot | I forgot my backup password. | We cannot open the backup or reset the password. This is why we ask you to keep it safe. |
| backup-restore | How do I restore a backup? | Settings, Restore from backup. Choose the file and enter its password. Your items are added as a new vault. Nothing on this iPhone is overwritten. |
| backup-pin | Is the backup password my PIN? | No. It is a separate password you choose when you export. |
| app-icon | Can I change the CipherRoll icon? | Yes. Settings, Privacy and lock, App icon. Choose CR lock, Calculator, Notes, Weather or Utility. iOS shows a short message when the icon changes. The name under the icon stays "CipherRoll". |
| video-temp | Does CipherRoll leave copies when I play a video? | To play a video, a protected temporary copy may be made on this iPhone. It is removed when you close the video and when the app starts. We cannot promise it is erased beyond recovery. |
| backup | Is there a backup? | Not yet. The Security and storage screen shows "Not backed up". Export a copy if the items matter to you. |
| analytics | Do you collect usage data? | Only if you allow it. On first launch we ask if CipherRoll may count anonymous app events (for example "paywall viewed") to improve the app. You can change this in Settings, then Privacy. We never send file names, photos, notes, PINs or search words. **[DEV CONFIRM]** |
| ads | Are there ads? | No. |

## Plans and payments
| Slug | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| free-plan | What do I get for free? | 1 vault, up to 100 photos, 10 videos, 10 files and 10 notes. Saving from other apps with Share also works on Free. |
| pro | What does Pro add? | Unlimited vaults and items, the decoy vault, and future updates to these features. Weekly, yearly and lifetime plans are available. |
| trial | How does the free trial work? | If you are eligible, the yearly plan starts with 7 days free. We remind you before it ends. You can cancel any time in iPhone Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. If you already used a trial, the screen shows the price with no trial. |
| renew | Do plans renew automatically? | Weekly ($1.99) and yearly ($19.99) plans renew automatically until you cancel. Lifetime ($39.99) is one payment with no renewal. Prices can differ by country. |
| cancel | How do I cancel? | iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, CipherRoll, Cancel. Or tap **Manage subscription** in CipherRoll. |
| after-trial | What happens when my trial or plan ends? | Nothing was deleted. 1 vault stays free and editable. Other vaults become read-only. Items above the free limit stay visible and exportable; you just cannot add more. |
| restore | I paid before. How do I restore? | Settings, Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID. This also works on a new iPhone. |
| refund | How do I get a refund? | Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com. |
| lifetime | What does Lifetime include? | One payment, Pro on your Apple ID. Updates to the Pro features are included. Separate future products, if any, are not promised. |

## Sharing into CipherRoll
| Slug | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| share-ext | Can I save things from other apps? | Yes, on the Free plan too. Tap Share in Photos, Files, Safari or other apps and choose CipherRoll. You unlock, pick a vault and save. |
